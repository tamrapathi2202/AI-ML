General.

(a) SOCP should be based on “Bolt on Container-on-Wheels” design and will be mounted on a
vehicle chassis provided by this unit.

(b) The “Container-on-Wheels” should be self-sufficient in providing Power, Communication and
Surveillance infrastructure for the command post and its surrounding area. For the comfort of
operators, SOCP will be fully Air Conditioned (Cooling & Heating) and should house ruggedized &
shock-vibration proof racks for housing electronic & communication equipment. Operator consoles
for the Communication & Surveillance video feed monitoring should also be ruggedized along with
Displays. Ruggedisation should be carried out for vibrations and movement along all three axis.

(c) An IP based Integrated Communication System (ICS) interfaced with various in service
wireless Radios should be supplied, thereby providing conferencing and patching facility between
multiple radio types or wireless frequencies along with Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

(Al/ML) retaining inherent secrecy of these radio sets.

(d) A single operator console should be able to display the Communication resources,
surveillance video feeds and GIS map data in form of overlay and transparencies.

(e) ICS and Surveillance System in SOCP should be able to communicate with the personnel
deployed with Radio on ground, away from SOCP as well as other ICS infrastructure and be able to

relay & receive data.

(f) Real-time video feeds from the deployed drones and helmet mounted cameras should also
be available. Apart from that, a wired and wireless backhaul connectivity capability should be

provided.
